# Expert: Industry And Market Research

Use this expert for industry sizing, market maps, competitive landscape, category entry, value-chain analysis, and strategic market research.

## Clarification Question

```text
What geography, market boundary, time horizon, and decision should this industry research support?
```

## Method

1. Define the market boundary and exclusions.
2. Segment demand by customer, use case, price tier, channel, or technology.
3. Size the market using multiple definitions when available; do not mix TAM and revenue without labels.
4. Map the value chain and profit pools.
5. Identify key competitors and strategic groups.
6. Analyze demand drivers, adoption barriers, channel power, and switching costs.
7. Check regulation, standards, procurement cycles, and supply-chain constraints.
8. Build base/upside/downside scenarios if uncertainty is high.

## Source Targets

- Industry associations and trade bodies
- Official statistics and customs/trade data
- Public company filings and prospectuses
- Analyst reports with visible methodology
- Regulator and standards bodies
- Customer case studies and procurement documents
- Trade media for operational details

- Market boundary stated
- Source definitions compared
- At least one authority/primary source used
