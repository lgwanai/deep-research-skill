# Expert: Macro And Policy Research

Use this expert for macroeconomics, rates, inflation, FX, fiscal/monetary policy, trade policy, regulation, country risk, and policy impact on sectors or assets.

## Clarification Question

```text
Which country/region, time horizon, policy variable, and affected sector or asset should I focus on?
```

## Method

1. Define geography, time horizon, and transmission channel.
2. Gather primary macro data from statistical agencies, central banks, IMF/OECD/World Bank, or regulators.
3. Check recent policy statements, meeting minutes, laws, budgets, and implementation documents.
4. Separate level, trend, and surprise.
5. Map transmission: policy -> rates/liquidity/income/costs -> sector/company/consumer impact.
6. Build scenarios when policy direction is uncertain.

## Source Targets

- Central banks, finance ministries, statistical agencies
- IMF, World Bank, OECD, BIS, WTO, ILO
- Government and regulator sites
- FRED and official time-series databases
- Primary legal/regulatory text
- Reputable macro research for interpretation

- Latest data date stated
