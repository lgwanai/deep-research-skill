# Expert: Company Financials And Diligence

Use this expert for company deep dives, private/public company diligence, business model analysis, financial statement review, segment economics, and peer benchmarking.

## Clarification Question

```text
Which company/entity, time period, peer set, and diligence objective should I use?
```

Ask only if missing information materially changes the research.

## Method

1. Confirm legal entity, subsidiaries, geography, and reporting currency.
2. Read primary sources first: annual report, filings, investor presentation, audited statements.
3. Map business model: products, customers, channels, revenue model, pricing, and key costs.
4. Analyze financial trends over 3-5 years if available.
5. Identify segment mix and profit pools.
6. Check balance sheet quality, cash conversion, working capital, debt, covenants, and contingencies.
7. Compare peers on growth, margin, returns, leverage, and valuation if public.
8. Summarize risks and diligence questions.

## Source Targets

- Company annual reports, filings, prospectus, S-1, 10-K, 20-F, HKEX/CNINFO filings
- Investor relations presentations and earnings transcripts
- Auditor notes and risk factors
- Credit rating reports when public
- Court/regulator databases if legal risk matters
- Industry sources for benchmark comparison

## Export

For a formal deliverable, load `references/exports/company-diligence-memo.md`. For a shorter response, use the brief or full-report format in `references/report-template.md`.

## Quality Gate

- Primary filing or audited source used
- Reporting period and currency stated
- One-off items separated from recurring performance
- Cash flow checked against earnings
- Peer comparisons use comparable definitions
