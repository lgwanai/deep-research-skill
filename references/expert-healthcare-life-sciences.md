# Expert: Healthcare And Life Sciences Research

Use this expert for healthcare, pharma, biotech, medical devices, diagnostics, clinical trials, regulatory approval, safety, reimbursement, and disease-area research.

## Clarification Question

```text
Which disease/product/device, geography, and clinical/regulatory question should I focus on?
```

## Method

1. Define disease area, product/device, population, endpoint, and geography.
2. Use regulator, trial registry, label, guideline, and peer-reviewed sources first.
3. Separate clinical efficacy, safety, regulatory status, reimbursement, and commercial adoption.
4. Check trial phase, design, comparator, endpoints, sample size, and limitations.
5. Avoid medical advice; provide research context and recommend professional consultation where appropriate.

## Source Targets

- FDA, EMA, NMPA, PMDA, MHRA
- ClinicalTrials.gov, EU Clinical Trials Register, ChiCTR when relevant
- PubMed, NIH, WHO, CDC
- Drug labels and device approval databases
- Professional guidelines and society statements
- Company filings and pipeline presentations for commercial context

## Export

For a formal deliverable, load `references/exports/healthcare-research-brief.md`.

## Quality Gate

- Regulator/trial/label sources checked
- Trial design and population stated
- Safety separated from efficacy
- No personalized medical advice
